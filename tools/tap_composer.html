Drum keys

How to use: Press R. You get a count-in, then the loop starts recording. Tap along with the keys below. Each lap of the loop layers more taps on top (overdub). Press R again to stop recording but keep it playing, or Enter to stop.

Notes mode (Tab): the middle row is a piano (A = C, black keys on W E T Y U O P), Z/X shift the octave. Space repeats the last note you played, so you can pick a pitch and then tap its rhythm with the spacebar.

Into BandLab: Export, then drag the .mid onto your BandLab project at the matching BPM. Drums come in on their own track (General MIDI channel 10), so pick a drum kit for it. Notes come in on a separate track.
